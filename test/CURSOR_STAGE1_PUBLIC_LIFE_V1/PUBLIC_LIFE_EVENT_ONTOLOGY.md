# PUBLIC_LIFE_V1 event ontology

Primary scoring unit: calendar year.

Each subject contributes exactly 2 positive and 2 negative distinct years.
Mixed events and positive/negative same-year collisions are not scored.

Positive: clear major award/championship, election/appointment, objective major career success, or clear legal reversal such as acquittal/release.

Negative: serious illness/treatment, major accident/injury, bankruptcy, conviction/imprisonment, involuntary major career loss, objective reputation loss with consequence, death, or major immediate-family bereavement.

Excluded by default: marriage/divorce alone, relocation, routine job change, ordinary project launch, vague career slump, undated mood descriptions, and normative judgments about whether a political policy itself was good/bad.

Evidence grade A = official/institutional or especially direct evidence.
Evidence grade B = reputable biography/reference or major media source.
Confidence medium is allowed only when the dated event is clear but whole-year valence is less direct.

No Control, V2, V3, astrology feature, Yongshin interpretation, predicted Daewoon, or model-correctness field is stored.
