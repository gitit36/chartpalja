Some stats about the champions In the 117 men's singles finals that have been played at the Monte-Carlo Masters between 1897 and 2026, a total of 67 players have won the title at least once . Among these champions, 25 won 2 or more times, 3 did it 5 or more times and just one of them lifted the trophy 10 times or more. On the other hand, 42 tennis players managed to win the Monte-Carlo Masters once in their careers and didn't repeat this achievement.
