The Monte-Carlo Masters has crowned its men's singles champion in 117 editions , in which a total of 67 players have won at least one title. The first champion was Reginald Doherty in 1897 and the most recent was Jannik Sinner in 2026. The last 4 editions of this tournament were won by 4 different players . In the last 10 editions, 6 players have won the title: Rafael Nadal (3), Stefanos Tsitsipas (3), Carlos Alcaraz, Fabio Fognini, Andrey Rublev and Jannik Sinner. Among the players that won titles , the most successful in the Monte-Carlo Masters is Rafael Nadal with 11 trophies . * the current ATP 250, 500 and 1000 categories were established in 1990 and their names have changed over the years.
