Her Story What We Stand For Our Products Cruelty-Free Books Connect Her Story About Suzanne Somers Suzanne Somers is one of America’s most popular and beloved personalities. In a multifaceted career that spanned more than three decades, she achieved extraordinary success as an actress, singer, comedienne, New York Times bestselling author, Las Vegas Entertainer of the Year, entrepreneur, and lecturer. She became the voice and face of alternative medicine—a fearless advocate who challenged conventional thinking about health, aging, and wellness. She received an Emmy nomination as Outstanding Host for “The SUZANNE Show,” her weekly Lifetime Network talk show, which provided a thought-provoking morning show alternative with in-depth information on health and wellness in a casual, entertaining format. Suzanne’s mission was simple: to help people live healthier, longer, more vibrant lives. Through her books, her products, and her tireless advocacy, she empowered millions to take control of their own health and make informed choices about their bodies.
