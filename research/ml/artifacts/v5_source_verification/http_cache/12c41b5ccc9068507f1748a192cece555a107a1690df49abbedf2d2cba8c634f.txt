The District Maryland Virginia Going Out Guide Obituaries John Diebold Dies Consultant Foresaw Computer Age December 28, 2005 More than 20 years ago Make us preferred on Google By Associated Press John Diebold, 79, a business visionary who preached computerization during the era of Elvis and Eisenhower as the future of worldwide industry, died of esophageal cancer Dec. 26 at his home in Bedford Hills, N.Y. Although Mr. Diebold is hailed as a prophet of the computerized future, his zeal for computers was not widely shared in the 1950s.
