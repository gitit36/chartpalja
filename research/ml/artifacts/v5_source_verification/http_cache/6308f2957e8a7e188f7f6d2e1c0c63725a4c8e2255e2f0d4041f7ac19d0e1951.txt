Georges Carpentier 1913-02-12 Paris, FRA Defeats Dick Rice to win IBU world and European titles; also wins European heavyweight title on 13-06-01; officially recognized by IBU at its convention on 20-02-05 ( The Sandusky Star-Journal , Sandusky, OH, USA, 20-02-06). Battling Siki 1922-09-24 Paris, FRA Wins all 3 titles; stripped by the French Federation of Boxing in 22-11; reinstated on 23-02-17 as the world light heavyweight and European heavyweight champion but European light heavyweight continues to be vacant. Louis Clement 1924-04-27 Geneva, SUI Swiss champion, defeats French champion Raymond Bonnel for the vacan European title. Herman van 't Hof 1926-01-05 Den Haag, NED Fernand Delarge 1926-07-25 Rotterdam, NED Max Schmeling 1927-06-19 Dortmund, GER Also holds German title; stripped of both titles in 28-05. Michele Bonaglia 1929-02-10 Milan, ITA Defeats Jack Etienne; vacant in 30. Ernst Pistulla 1931-03-18 Valencia, ESP Defeats José Martinez de Alfara; vacant in 32(?). Adolf Heuser 1932-06-25 Valencia, ESP Defeats José Martinez de Alfara; vacant in 33. John Andersson 1933-04-01 Brussels, BEL Defeats Jack Etienne; vacates in 34-01(?). Martinez de Alfara 1934-02-07 Barcelona, ESP Defeats Leonard Steyaert. Marcel Thil 1934-03-26 Paris, FRA Also holds IBU world middleweight title; also wins European middleweight title on 34-05-03; vacates the light heavyweight title on 35-05-22. Merlo Preciso 1935-08-09 Munich, BY, GER Defeats Adolf Witt. Heinz Lazek 1935-09-17 Vienna, AUT Gustave Roth 1936-09-01 Vienna, AUT Adolf Heuser [2] 1938-03-25 Berlin, GER Stripped of the title for being overweight. Luigi Musina 1942-04-05 Berlin, GER Defeats Richard Vogt; vacant in 47 (sometime between 47-02 and 47-08). Freddie Mills 1947-09-08 London, ENG, GBR Defeats Pol Goffaux; vacates on 50-02-15 upon retirement. Albert Yvel 1950-07-09 Algiers, ALG Defeats Renato Tontini. Don Cockell 1951-03-27 London, ENG, GBR Vacant in 52(?). Conny Rux 1952-07-26 Berlin, GER Defeats Willy Schagen; vacant in 53(?). Jacques Hairabedian 1953-07-12 Rome, ITA Defeats Renato Tontini. Gerhard Hecht 1954-04-09 Hamburg, GER Willi Hoepner 1955-03-11 Hamburg, GER Gerhard Hecht 1955-06-12 Dortmund, GER Artemio Calzavara 1957-07-12 Milan, ITA Willi Hoepner 1958-05-30 Hamburg, GER Erich Schoppner 1958-12-12 Hamburg, GER Vacant in 62. Giulio Rinaldi 1962-09-28 Rome, ITA Defeats Chic Calderwood. Gustav Scholz 1964-04-04 Dortmund, GER Vacates in 64 upon retirement. Giulio Rinaldi [2] 1965-07-08 Rome, ITA Defeats Klaus Peter Gumpert. Rudi Koopmans 1979-03-07 Rotterdam, NED Richard Caramanolis 1984-02-02 Marseille, FRA Alex Blanchard 1984-05-28 Amsterdam, NED Tom Collins 1987-11-11 Usk, WEL, GBR Pedro van Raamsdonk 1988-09-07 Reading, ENG, GBR Jan Lefeber 1988-11-07 Rotterdam, NED Eric Nicoletta 1989-10-13 Sète, FRA Tom Collins [2] 1990-08-11 Hérault, FRA Vacant in 91. Clinton Woods 1999-03-13 Manchester, ENG, GBR Wins European, Commonwealth , and British titles; vacates in 01, probably after winning WBC international title on 01-03-24. Yawe Davis 2001-04-06 Toscana, ITA Defeats Neil Simpson. Thomas Ulrich 2002-10-12 Schwerin, GER Vacant in 02(?). Stipe Drews 2003-02-08 Berlin, GER Defeats Silvio Branco; vacant in 04. Nathan Cleverly 2010-02-13 London, ENG, GBR Defeats Antonio Brancalion; wins WBO interim world title on 10-12-11. Danny McIntosh 2011-01-22 Levallois-Perret, FRA Defeats Thierry Karl. Eduard Gutknecht 2011-05-07 Neubrandenburg, GER Jürgen Brähmer 2013-02-02 Berlin, GER Vacates the title after winning WBO interim world title on 09-08-22. Karo Murat 2017-07-01 Dresden, GER Defeats Dominic Boesel; vacant in 08. Dominic Boesel 2017-10-27 Weissenfels, GER Defeats Serhiy Demchenko; wins IBO World and interim WBA World titles on 19-11-16. Mathieu Bauderlique 2021-09-10 Paris, FRA Defeats Igor Mikhalkin; loses a non-title match on 22-08-20. Dan Azeez 2023-03-11 Paris, FRA Defeats Thomas Faure; also holds Commonwealth and British titles but loses both of them on 24-02-03. Daniel Blenda Dos Santos 2024-06-15 Pont-Sainte-Maxence, Oise, FRA Defeats Thomas Faure; stripped of the title on 25-04-10 after Does Santos is unable to fight the scheduled title defense on 25-04-04 due to illness. Bradley Rea 2025-06-28 Hull, Yorkshire, ENG, GBR Defeats Shakan Pitters.
