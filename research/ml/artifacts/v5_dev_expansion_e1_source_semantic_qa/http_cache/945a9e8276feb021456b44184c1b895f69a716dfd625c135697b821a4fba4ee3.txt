He acted with Robert DeNiro in Jacknife (1989), with Tom Hanks in Apollo 13 (1995) by Ron Howard, with Anthony Hopkins in Nixon (1995) by Oliver Stone, with Jim Carrey in The Truman Show (1998) by Peter Weir, with Sean Connery and Nicolas Cage in The Rock (1996) by Michael Bay, with Russell Crowe in A Beautiful Mind (2001) by Ron Howard, with Meryl Streep, Julianne Moore, Nicole Kidman in The Hours (2002) by Stephen Daldry, with Viggo Mortensen in A History of Violence (2005) by David Cronenberg, with Casey Affleck in Gone Baby Gone (2007) directed by Ben Affleck. Harris directed and starred in Pollock (2000) about the life of painter Jackson Pollock, in the western Appaloosa (2008) with Viggo Mortensen and Renée Zellweger. He acted in The Way Back (2010) by Peter Weir, Frontera (2014), Snowpiercer (2014) by Bong Joon-ho, Kodachrome (2017), The Lost Daughter (2021) directed by Maggie Gyllenhaal from the 2006 novel by Elena Ferrante, Top Gun: Maverick (2022) with Tom Cruise. On television he acted in Empire Falls (2005), Game Change (2012), Westworld (2016-2022) based on the 1973 movie by Michael Crichton.
