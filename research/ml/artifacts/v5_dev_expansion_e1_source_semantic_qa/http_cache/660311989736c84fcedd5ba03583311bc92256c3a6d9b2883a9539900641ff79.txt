Mary-Kate and Ashley became co-presidents on their their 18th birthday in 2004, and in 2007 they were named the eleventh-richest women in entertainment by Forbes .During their teens, they starred in a string of films together until 2004's New York Minute - their final joint production, and Ashley's last acting role to date.Since moving away from Hollywood, the Olsens' focus has turned to fashion. They founded The Row in 2006, named after London's Savile Row, followed by a more casual line in 2007, Elizabeth and James. If confirmation of their fashion status was needed, in 2012 they were first nominated for the prestigious CFDA Womenswear Designer of the Year award alongside fashion heavyweights Marc Jacobs and the Proenza Schouler designers, Lazaro Hernandez and Jack McCollough - they won the title in 2015.
