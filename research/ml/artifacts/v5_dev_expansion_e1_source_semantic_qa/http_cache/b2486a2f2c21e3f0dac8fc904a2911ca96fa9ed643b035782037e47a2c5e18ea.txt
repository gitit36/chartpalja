(TBS, Inc.) and purchases the Atlanta Braves baseball team; purchases first bison (goes on to amass a herd of 51,000) 1977 1977 Wins the America’s Cup; purchases the Atlanta Hawks basketball team 1979 1979 Announces his plans to launch a groundbreaking 24-hour news network titled Cable News Network (CNN) 1979 Turner and his crew are feared dead Turner and his crew are feared dead as their yacht Tenacious remains unaccounted for after encountering disastrous weather during the 605-mile Fastnet ocean race. Tenacious goes on to win Fastnet race 1980 1980 Officially launches CNN – June 1, 1980 1982 1982 Launches another news channel with a thirty-minute cycle of headline news stories titled CNN2 (later to become known as Headline News) 1986 1986 Hosts the first Goodwill Games held in Moscow, Russia 1987 1987 Purchases first Western property – The Bar None Ranch Purchases first Western property The Bar None Ranch, going on to become one of the nation’s largest individual landowners with 2 million acres 1988 1988 Launches Turner Network Television (TNT) 1990 1990 Establishes the Turner Foundation, Inc. and launches the Captain Planet and Planeteers animated series Establishes the Turner Foundation, Inc., a private, independent family foundation committed to preventing damage to the natural systems – water, air, and land; Created by Ted Turner and Barbara Pyle, the animated series Captain Planet and the Planeteers airs on TBS and receives international acclaim; the Captain Planet Foundation (CPF) is established the following year 1991 1991 CNN establishes itself as the world’s leader in news; Ted Turner is named Man of the Year by Time Magazine 1992 1992 Launches the Cartoon Network 1994 1994 Launches Turner Classic Movies (TCM) 1995 1995 Under Ted Turner’s ownership, the Atlanta Braves win the World Series 1996 1996 Turner Broadcasting System, Inc. merges with Time Warner, Inc.
